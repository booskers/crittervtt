A game table everyone
can sit at, online.

Critter is a free virtual tabletop. Share a lobby code and your group is in, with the same battle map, dice, character sheets and rulebooks, and every roll as it happens.

Play in your browser Download for Windows

Free and open source. Nothing to install to play in a browser.

Features

Everything the table needs.

Everyone at the table sees the same thing at the same moment: maps, rolls, sheets and music.

  • A battle map with tokens, drawing tools and each player's live cursor.
  • Dice from d4 to d100, with modifiers, hidden GM rolls and typed rolls like 2d6+3.
  • Character sheets and items, with D&D Beyond import for the owner of a lobby.
  • Built-in rules compendium for D&D 5e, Pathfinder 2e, Daggerheart, Blades in the Dark and Fate.
  • Autosaves and Rewind on the server, plus the GM's own saves folder on their computer.
  • Works on phones, in any modern browser, or as a Windows app.

Players and seats

The owner sets up the players; each person claims theirs with a name, token and colour.

Library and creators

Spells, monsters, items and classes, plus importers and creature builders.

Rewind the table

Saves every ten minutes while you play. Rewind a lost evening, and undo the rewind too.

Browser or desktop

The same table in a browser, or in the Windows app with its own window.

Host it yourself

Your group, your server.

Critter connects through Homebase, a small server that keeps your tables and pushes every change live. Run your own in one of two ways.

On Cloudflare

A Worker on your own free Cloudflare account. One double-click on setup-homebase.cmd puts it online and builds an installer that connects to it.

On your computer

A Node server: node server.mjs --port 8787. Players open its address in a browser, or point the app at it.

Your data

Tables, autosaves and uploaded music stay on your server, in a folder you can back up.

The full guide is in the project README.

Pairs with Critter Sounds

Music, played live to the table.

Critter Sounds is the companion app for whoever runs the music. In Critter, the owner opens Music, copies the table's music code and pastes it into Critter Sounds. Everyone hears the music, and each player sets their own volume.
