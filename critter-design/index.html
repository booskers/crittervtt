Critter design system · draft 3

One look for Critter, Sounds and Notes

Surfaces lift by light and shadow, not 1px outlines. Pictures spill their colour into the space around them, and things glow only when you point at them. Each app keeps its own colours; all three share one set of fonts, and every choice below is yours to change.

Artwork effects

Pick a picture: the Sounds play bar and the Notes page follow it.
Bleed
Colour spills out of the art

Blurred, saturated and graded into a safe band, then masked away from the picture. All text on it is ink. For the play bar, sheet headers, roll cards and page washes.

Glow on hover

Point at a picture: a blurred copy appears under it as a coloured glow. Nothing glows until you hover.

Banner

A wide picture that fades into the surface below it. For page headers in Notes and scene cards in Critter.

Fonts

Easy reading is the base for all three apps. Each app's settings offer these pairings (try them per app with the picker beside each app's name); each role can also be picked on its own.

Components

The same parts in each app's colours and fonts, with text on every surface level.

Any colour stays readable

Every preset from the three apps, and some extremes, as accent text, solid fill and soft chip.
